# Mapping Safety, Danger, and Visibility

## Historical Question
Did gay safe places increase between 1965 and 1985, or did they become more visible, more contested, and differently represented depending on the source?

## Sources
- Damron Guides dataset
- New York Times Archive API outsider dataset

## Methodology
- Identify cruising locations in Damron
- Identify HOT/AYOR danger labels
- Build NYT outsider dataset from keyword searches
- Categorize NYT keywords
- Normalize Damron and NYT annual scores
- Compare insider and outsider perspectives

## Key Findings
- Damron suggests queer spaces were being mapped with increasing detail and risk labels.
- NYT shows mainstream discourse often framed homosexuality through crime, rights, morality, public space, and crisis.
- The normalized score shows when insider danger and outsider discourse rose together.
- The perspective gap shows when the two archives disagreed.

## Limitations
- Keyword choice shapes the result.
- Damron and NYT measure different things.
- NYT is not a direct measure of safe places.
- NLP amplifies the perspective of the archive.
